About

I was born and raised in Sri Lanka, where I spent much of my time outdoors, whether playing baseball at school or simply exploring and enjoying nature. Looking back, I’m glad I had those experiences because I believe they helped shape me into a creative person who is always open to new challenges.

I have a bachelor’s degree in Chemical Engineering and am currently a final-year master’s student in Industrial Design Engineering. I’m passionate about exploring chemical engineering and related fields to solve problems at the intersection of chemical engineering, the environment, and health.

Beyond engineering, I’m interested in technology and design. I see technology as a way to stay up to date with the world, while design helps me stay creative and look at things from different, sometimes unconventional, perspectives. I enjoy building new things, drawing, and watching movies. I also love picking up new skills and gradually mastering them in my free time. When I’m not doing any of those things, you’ll probably find me reading about interesting discoveries in other fields, such as health and technology, or getting lost in science fiction, where I often find fascinating ideas.
